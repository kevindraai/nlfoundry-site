_Last updated: 4 August 2026_

This page is a deliberately small, public snapshot of what N/L Foundry is actively shaping. It is not a promise of release dates and does not expose private infrastructure or internal work orders.

## Current focus

### ClubPOS

Building the product foundations for a hosted point-of-sale platform aimed at clubs and associations. Current work centres on a reliable domain model, exact product-unit calculations, auditable flows and the future separation between organisations.

### ExitLane

Improving the operator experience around network-wide VPN routing, deployment, health information and predictable recovery behaviour.

### N/L Foundry engineering

Turning lessons from active projects into a repeatable way of working with explicit quality gates, reproducible infrastructure and clear responsibilities between people, automation and AI-assisted tools.

### Public presence

Establishing this website as the shared front door for products, documentation and selected engineering articles.

## Recently established

- the first public N/L Foundry website foundation;
- a shared product positioning that supports both open-source and hosted software;
- reproducible Debian and Proxmox practices for development environments;
- public engineering and AI-transparency principles.

## Next on the public side

The next useful additions are real product screenshots, release-aware project pages and the first Engineering Journal article. These will be added when they can describe working software rather than aspirations.

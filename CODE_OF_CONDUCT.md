# Code of Conduct

N/L Foundry welcomes contributions that are respectful, inclusive, and professional.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful in all communication.
- Critique work, not people.
- Keep collaboration constructive and focused on quality.
- Keep language and feedback suitable for a public community space.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, hate speech, or threats.
- Repeated spam or disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Community moderators may remove abusive comments and ask disruptive participants to stop. Violations may result in loss of contribution privileges.
